དགག་ཚིག

དགག་ཚིག are Dzongkha's negative words — the equivalent of "no," "not," and "don't" in English. The main ones are མ, མི, མེན, and མེད.

Examples

ཆུ་མེདNo water
ང་མེནNot me
མ་བརྐོdon't/didn't dig
མི་...ཉོwill not buy

Notice that these negative words attach directly onto the word they're negating, right where you'd expect "not" to sit in the English translation.
