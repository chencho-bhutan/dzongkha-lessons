སྤྱི་སྒྲ

སྤྱི་སྒྲ are general-purpose question words — the Dzongkha equivalent of "who," "what," "where," "when," and "how."

The Question Words

གwho
ག་ཅིwhat
ག་ཏེwhere
ནམwhen
ག་དེ་སྦེhow
ག་དེམhow many

In the more formal Choekey style of Dzongkha, the equivalent words are སུ, ཇི, གང, ཅི, and དེ.

Examples

ག་སྨོ། ག་གིས་བྲིས་ཡིWho wrote it?
ག་ཅི་འབག་འོང་ཡི། ག་ཅི་ཨིན་མསWhat did [they] bring? What is it?
ག་ཏེ་ལུ་སོང་ཡི། ག་ཏེ་ཡང་མིན་འདུགWhere did [they] go? [They're] nowhere.
ནམ་འབད་རུང་བཏུབ། ནམ་དགོ་ནིAnytime is fine. When is it needed?
ག་དེ་སྦེ་འདུག། ག་དེ་འབད་རུང་བཏུབHow is it? However [it's done] is fine.
