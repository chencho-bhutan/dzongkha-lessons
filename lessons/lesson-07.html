The Three Dogchen — འདོགས་ཅན་གསུམ

If Gochen letters sit on top, Dogchen (འདོགས་ཅན) letters do the opposite: they attach underneath a base letter.

The word Dogchen literally means "letters that are propped up from below." There are three letters that can attach underneath another letter this way: ཡ (ya), ར (ra), and ལ (la). Just like the Gochen, each one has its own specific list of base letters it can attach to.

Ya-tag — Seven Letters with ཡ Underneath

ཡ་བཏགས་བདུན — "ya-tag duen"

ཀྱ · ཁྱ · གྱ · པྱ · ཕྱ · བྱ · མྱ

One Letter, Two Roles

Some individual letters show up in both the Gochen lists and the Dogchen lists. That's not a mistake — it means that particular letter is capable of being stacked on top of some letters, and attached underneath other letters, just in different combinations. It's the same base letter doing two different jobs depending on the word.
