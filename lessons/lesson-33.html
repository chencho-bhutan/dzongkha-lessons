རྣམ་དབྱེ་དང་པ — Simply Naming a Thing

This is the simplest of the eight sentence types. It works like a plain noun in English: it names a person, animal, thing, or place (or a pronoun standing in for one), with no adjective describing it and no action attached.

Examples

འབྲུགBhutan
ཐིམ་ཕུགThimphu
ཕུན་ཚོགསPhuntsho (a name)
སྟགTiger
རྐང་ཁྲིA stool / bench

Each of these is just a bare name — nothing added, nothing described, no action taking place. It's the starting point every other sentence type builds on.
