རྣམ་དབྱེ་དྲུག་པ — Connective Case

This sentence type expresses a relationship between two things — oneness, possession, belonging, a whole and its parts, a thing and its source, or a subject and its properties.

Helping word used: ཀྱི, གི, གྱི, འི, ཡི

Examples

གསེར་གྱི་ཐུརམA ring made of gold.
བསང་གི་དུ་པSmoke from burning incense.
གངས་ཀྱི་སེང་གེThe snow lion.
ཤིང་གི་འདབ་མA leaf of a tree.
བུ་མོའི་རྣམ་འགྱུརA girl's expression / demeanor.
དཔོན་གྱི་ཕྱག་མཛོདThe lord's treasurer.

This is the same job "'s" or "of" does in English — linking two nouns together to show that one belongs to, is made of, or is associated with the other.
