མིང་གི་ཚིག — Noun

མིང་གི་ཚིག is the Dzongkha word for noun — a word that names a person, place, or thing.

Examples

གཡང་སྒྲོནYangdon (a name)
གླངOx
སྣུམ་འཁོརCar
ཐིམ་ཕུགThimphu

Nouns cover the widest possible range of things: people's names, animals, everyday objects, and places — as you can see from the mix of examples above.
