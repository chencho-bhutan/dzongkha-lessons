ལ་དོན

ལ་དོན covers seven helping words — སུ, ར, རུ, དུ, ཏུ, ན, and ལ — used like prepositions of time and place in English (words such as "at," "in," "to," or "on").

Which One to Use

  • སུ follows the Jejug letter ས
  • ཏུ follows the Jejug letters ག, བ, ན, ར, ལ
  • དུ follows the Jejug letters ང, ན, མ, ར, ལ
  • ན or ལ follow any ending letter
  • ར or རུ follow འ, or no ending letter at all

Examples

གནས་སུ · རྟག་ཏུ · གང་དུ · སྡུག་ན / སྡུག་ལ · འདི་རུ / འདིར

Each of these attaches to a word to show where or when something is happening — much like English prepositions do.
