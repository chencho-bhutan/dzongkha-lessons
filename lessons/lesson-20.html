འབྲེལ་སྒྲ

འབྲེལ་སྒྲ covers five helping words — ཀྱི, གྱི, གི, འི, and ཡི — used to connect words or phrases, often to show possession (similar to "'s" or "of" in English).

Which One to Use

  • ཀྱི follows the Jejug letters ད, བ, ས
  • གྱི follows the Jejug letters ན, མ, ར, ལ
  • གི follows the Jejug letters ག, ང
  • འི or ཡི follows འ, or no ending letter at all

Examples

ཁྱོད་ཀྱི · མགྱོནམ་གྱི · རང་གི · མཁའི

Each of these works the way "'s" works in English — turning a noun into a possessive form, so you can say something belongs to someone or something.
