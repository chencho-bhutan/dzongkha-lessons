རྣམ་དབྱེ་གཉིས་པ — The Object of an Action

This sentence type clearly describes work being done, without necessarily saying who's doing it.

Helping word used: ན, ར, ལུ, གུ

Examples

ནང་ན་སྡོད།Stay at home.
གཡུལ་ཁར་སོང་།[He/she] went to the countryside.
དཔེ་ལུ་བལྟ།Look at the book.
གདོང་གུ་དབུར།Wash [your] face.

Notice how each sentence tells you exactly what's being done — staying, going, looking, washing — and to or at what, but doesn't tell you who's doing it. That's the defining feature of this sentence type.
