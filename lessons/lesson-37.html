རྣམ་དབྱེ་ལྔ་པ — Source / Origin

This sentence type shows where something comes from — the source, reason, or cause behind a thing, idea, or situation.

Helping word used: ལས

Examples

བ་ལས་ཨོམ།Milk comes from a cow.
ཨའི་ལས་ཨ་ལུ།A child comes from [its] mother.

Both examples follow the same shape: [source] + ལས + [thing that comes from it]. It's the direct equivalent of saying "X comes from Y" in English.
