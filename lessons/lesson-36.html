རྣམ་དབྱེ་བཞི་པ — Purpose / Recipient

This sentence type shows the purpose behind an action — the reason something is done, or who benefits from it.

Helping word used: ར, དང, ལུ

Examples

དབང་ཞུ་བར་སོང་།[He/she] went to receive an empowerment.
སྨན་བྱིན་པར་སོང་།[He/she] went to give medicine.
སེམས་ཅན་གྱི་དོན་ལུ་སངས་རྒྱས་བསྒྲུབ།[One] attains enlightenment for the sake of sentient beings.
མི་ཉམས་ཆུང་ལུ་བརྩེ་བ་འབད།Show compassion to the weak and vulnerable.

Every one of these sentences answers the question "for what purpose?" or "for whose benefit?" — going somewhere to receive a blessing, giving medicine to help someone, and so on.
