རྒྱན་སྡུད

རྒྱན་སྡུད covers three helping words — ཀྱང, འང, and ཡང — used to build phrases or sentences that express similarity, contradiction, or a summary (roughly like "even," "also," or "although" in English).

Which One to Use

  • ཀྱང follows the Jejug letters ག, ད, བ, ས
  • ཡང follows the Jejug letters ང, ན, མ, ར, ལ
  • འང follows འ, or no ending letter at all

Examples

ཐོག་ཀྱང · མཆོད་ཀྱང · རུང་ཡང · ལན་ཡང
