ཕྲད — Helping Verbs in Dzongkha

You've now met the main words. But a pile of main words sitting next to each other isn't a sentence — you need something to link them together, the way "and," "that," or "because" work in English.

In Dzongkha, these linking words are called ཕྲད and རྐྱེན. They're used to build sentences and paragraphs by joining different parts of speech into a single, logical idea.

Two Types of Helping Words

Helping words in Dzongkha split into two groups:

  1. Words that follow rules — these can only attach after certain specific letters (you'll recognize these letters from the Jejug list you learned earlier).
  2. Words that don't follow rules — these can attach more freely, without the same letter restrictions.

Each type of helping word does a specific job — some link related ideas together, some show similarity or contrast, some ask questions, and some mark the end of a sentence. Over the next several lessons, we'll go through each type one at a time, starting with the ones that link a phrase to what follows it.
