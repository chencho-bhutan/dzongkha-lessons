མིང་གི་ཁྱད་ཚིག — Adjective

མིང་གི་ཁྱད་ཚིག is the Dzongkha word for adjective — a word that describes a quality of a noun, like its size, color, or feel.

Examples

འཇའ་རིསམོBeautiful
སྦོམBig
དཀརཔོWhite
བསིལ་ཏོང་ཏོCool

A Quick Recap

You've now met all five main word categories in Dzongkha:

  • བྱ་ཚིག — Verb (an action)
  • དབྱེ་ཚིག — Adverb (describes how an action happens)
  • མིང་གི་ཚིག — Noun (a person, place, or thing)
  • མིང་ཚབ — Pronoun (stands in for a noun)
  • མིང་གི་ཁྱད་ཚིག — Adjective (describes a noun's quality)

These are the "main" words — the ones that carry the actual meaning of a sentence. But main words alone don't make a complete sentence. You need something to connect them.
