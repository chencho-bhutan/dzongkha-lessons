བྱ་ཚིག — Verb

Dzongkha words fall into two broad groups, much like English does: main words (nouns, pronouns, verbs, adverbs, adjectives, and so on) and helping words, which join the main words together into sentences.

We'll start with the main words, and the first one is the verb. བྱ་ཚིག is simply the Dzongkha word for verb — a word that describes an action.

Examples

བྱིནgive
འགྱོgo
བརྐོdig
ཉལsleep

What's Next

On its own, a verb like འགྱོ ("go") doesn't tell you when something happens — whether it already happened, is happening now, or hasn't happened yet. In English, we handle that with words like "went," "going," and "will go." Dzongkha has its own version of this, and we'll cover it later in the course, once we've looked at how full sentences and tenses work.

For now, we continue with the next main word category: the adverb.
