ལྷག་བཅས

ལྷག་བཅས covers three helping words — སྟེ, ཏེ, and དེ — that all do the same basic job: connecting one phrase to the phrase that follows and completes it, showing that the two are related or dependent on each other.

Which One to Use

Each of the three words attaches after a specific set of Jejug (ending) letters:

  • སྟེ follows the Jejug letters ག, ང, བ, མ, འ, or no ending letter at all
  • ཏེ follows the Jejug letters ན, ར, ལ, ས
  • དེ follows any other Jejug letter not covered above

Examples

སྐྱོ་སྟེ · གྱོན་ཏེ · བསྐོར་ཏེ · སྡོད་དེ

In Full Sentences

  • མལ་ཆ་བཀབ་སྟེ་དྲོ
  • གདོང་འཁྱུ་སྟེ་སྨན་དབུར
  • ལེའུ་བསྒྱུར་ཏེ་ཞབས་བྲོ་རྐྱབ

Each of these follows the same shape: a first phrase, then one of our three helping words, then a second phrase that naturally continues or completes the first.
