རྣམ་དབྱེ་བདུན་པ — Location Case

This sentence type shows preposition of place and time — where or when something is happening.

Helping word used: ན, ར, ལུ, གུ

Examples

ཤིང་གུ་བྱA bird on the tree.
མགུ་ཏོ་ཁར་ཞྭམོA hat on [my/the] head.
བར་ན་སྡོདStay in the middle.
གཟའ་ཟླཝ་ལུ་ཤོགCome on Monday.
ལོ་འབག་གུ་ཀྲིག་ཀྲིག་ཕོག་ནུགHail struck hard on the crops.
གནམ་བསྲོ་ཁམས་ཅིག་ཁར་ལྷོད་ཅི།[We] arrived at a time when the weather had turned warm.

Each of these tells you either where something is (on the tree, on the head, in the middle) or when something happens (on Monday, at a certain time) — the same job prepositions like "on," "in," and "at" do in English.
