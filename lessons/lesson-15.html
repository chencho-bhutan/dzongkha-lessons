མིང་ཚབ — Pronoun

མིང་ཚབ is the Dzongkha word for pronoun — a word used in place of the actual name of a person, animal, thing, or place, so you don't have to keep repeating it.

Examples

ཁོHe
མོShe
ཁོངThey
ངI

Just like in English, once you've mentioned a person or thing by name once, a pronoun lets you refer back to them without repeating the full name every time.
