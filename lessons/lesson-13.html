དབྱེ་ཚིག — Adverb

དབྱེ་ཚིག is the Dzongkha word for adverb — a word that gives more information about a verb, telling you how an action is done.

Examples

ཁུ་སིམ་སིམ་སྦེ་སྡོད།Stay quietly
མགྱོགས་པར་འགྱོ།Go fast
ཧིང་སངས་སངས་སྦེ་འབྲི།Write clearly

Notice the pattern in each phrase: the adverb comes first, describing how the action should be carried out, and the verb (stay, go, write) follows it.
